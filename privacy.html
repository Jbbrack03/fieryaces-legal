Fiery Aces Privacy Policy

Nimblor LLC · Effective November 16, 2026

Fiery Aces is an arcade dogfighting game published by Nimblor LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. The short version: we collect only what is needed to run online matches and save your online progress. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it lives
A random player ID and a guest sign-in key created on your device To give you an online pilot without asking for an email or password The key stays on your device; our servers store only a one-way hash of it
Your call sign (the pilot name you choose) Shown to other players in online matches and results Our servers and your device
Online progression: level, experience, in-game currency (Marks), unlocked planes and parts, and match results such as kills and time played To save and restore your online progress Our servers
When your account was created and last signed in To keep the service working and spot abuse Our servers
Your IP address and network timing (ping) Needed to connect you to a match server and pick a nearby one Used while you play; may appear in server logs kept up to 14 days
Game settings, offline progress and medals Your preferences and offline play Only on your device; never sent to us

We do not collect your real name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifiers or payment details. Offline, LAN and bot matches do not send anything to our servers.

How it is processed

Online services run on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States, which processes this data on our behalf. We use it only to operate the game: signing you in, matchmaking, running matches, awarding progression and preventing cheating. Other players in a match can see your call sign and in-match stats.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and online progression: until you delete your account.
  • Records used to prevent duplicate match rewards: 7 days.
  • Server logs: up to 14 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your online account at any time in the game: Options › Account › Delete Account. This permanently removes your player ID, call sign and online progression from our servers right away; any remaining duplicate-reward records expire within 7 days, and logs within 14 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device. If you can no longer open the game, contact us and we will delete your account for you.

Children

Fiery Aces does not ask for personal information such as names, emails or contact details from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data. Use the in-game delete option or contact us, and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and tell players in the game about significant changes.
